## Context

South Sumatra Province needed its electronic-government architecture grounded in an accurate
picture of how its agencies actually work. Before any architecture could be drawn, the raw
business process data scattered across more than thirty regional agencies (OPD) had to be
consolidated into something an analyst could reason about.

## What I did

- Consolidated and structured business process data collected from **30+ regional agencies**
- Mapped each agency to its governmental functions (*urusan*), across the full set of organizations
- Produced an analysis-ready data structure serving as the foundation for provincial SPBE
  architecture planning and data governance

## Outcome

The province moved into its architecture design phase with a single, consistent process
inventory rather than thirty disconnected agency submissions, removing the reconciliation work
that usually consumes the first month of an SPBE engagement.
