## Context

At LKPP the hard part was not the framework. It was getting consistent data out of divisions
that each had their own way of describing what they hold. That made facilitation as important
as analysis.

## What I did

- Ran cross-division data collection across the agency
- Served as **speaker at a multi-deputy data collection event**, aligning divisions on what was
  being asked and why before collection began
- Classified data assets across **40+ organizational units**

## Outcome

Bringing the deputies into one room ahead of collection cut the back-and-forth that usually
follows inconsistent submissions, and produced a classification the divisions recognized as
their own.
