## Context

PPATK handles financial intelligence data, where classification and security design are not
documentation exercises. They determine who may touch what. The engagement applied DMBOK to an
institution with a high sensitivity floor.

## What I did

- Classified data assets across **40+ organizational units**
- Authored **40+ operational standards** governing those data assets
- Designed the data security scheme covering access and handling
- Delivered reporting structured around the **DMBOK framework**

## Outcome

The agency ended with data assets classified, standardized, and paired with a security scheme
built on an internationally recognized framework rather than local convention.
