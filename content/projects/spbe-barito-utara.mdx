## Context

Barito Utara needed a documented architecture to anchor its digitalization direction — and
someone to lead the documentation effort across every regional agency rather than contribute to
one part of it.

## What I did

- **Led** data-driven architecture documentation across **30+ regional agencies (OPD)**
- Produced **100+ service architecture codes**
- Produced **500+ data architecture codes**
- Produced **200+ application architecture codes**
- Delivered the **as-is to to-be analysis** setting the regency's digitalization direction

## Outcome

The regency's digitalization plan rests on documented current-state architecture rather than
assumption — with the gap to the target state expressed in coded, trackable terms.
