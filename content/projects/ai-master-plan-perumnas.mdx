## Context

Perumnas needed more than a list of AI tools. It needed a defensible position on where
artificial intelligence fits into the organization over the next five years, and what has to be
true internally before any of it works.

## What I did

- Authored a **five-year AI strategic master plan** aligned with the Generative AI landscape
- Designed training programs targeted at **6+ critical organizational roles**
- Developed **2+ chatbot conversation flow prototypes** to make the strategy tangible
- Built the implementation roadmap and mapped **3+ advanced Generative AI technologies**
  against concrete organizational use cases

## Outcome

Leadership received a plan that sequences capability building before tooling, training the
roles that would own AI systems first, then phasing technology adoption against a roadmap
rather than a procurement cycle.
