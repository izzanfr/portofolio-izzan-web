## Context

Jepara Regency needed its service architecture documented from the ground up — what each agency
delivers, which data those services consume and produce, and which applications already exist to
support them.

## What I did

- Conducted desk research into the business processes of **30+ regional agencies (OPD)**
- Compiled a service inventory for each agency
- Mapped data utilization across services, connecting processes to the data they depend on
- Documented the regency's application inventory

## Outcome

The regency gained a linked view of processes, services, data, and applications — the
prerequisite for identifying duplicate systems and unserved functions before new procurement.
