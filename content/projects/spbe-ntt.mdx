## Context

A provincial SPBE roadmap is only as good as the field data behind it. NTT's engagement began
with survey work across the province's regional agencies, covering three architecture domains
at once: services, data, and applications.

## What I did

- Collected and analyzed service, data, and application data from **30+ regional agencies (OPD)**
- Standardized incoming data so submissions from different agencies became comparable
- Designed the documentation format used by the surveyor team, keeping collection consistent
  across the field
- Built a comprehensive database supporting East Nusa Tenggara's digital transformation planning

## Outcome

The roadmap team worked from one standardized database instead of raw agency-by-agency
responses, which made cross-agency gaps visible rather than buried in formatting differences.
